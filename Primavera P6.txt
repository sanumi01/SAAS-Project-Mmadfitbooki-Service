🧭 Step-by-Step Roadmap to Learn Primavera P6
🔹 1. Understand the Basics of Project Management
Before diving into Primavera, ensure you're familiar with core concepts like:

Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

Gantt charts

Critical Path Method (CPM)

Resources & Cost management

Baselines and Variance analysis

🔸 Suggested resources:

PMBOK Guide (Project Management Institute)

Free YouTube channels: “Project Management Simplified,” “PMC Lounge”

🔹 2. Get Access to Primavera P6
Options:

Trial Version from Oracle (Oracle sometimes offers it for learning/demo)

Use a cloud-based version via third-party resellers (e.g., CBA, Ten Six)

Download a cracked version is illegal and not recommended

If access is an issue, you can use Microsoft Project or ProjectLibre to practice similar concepts.

🔹 3. Follow a Beginner Tutorial
Focus first on navigation, terminology, and basic functions:

Creating a project

Defining WBS

Adding activities

Assigning relationships (predecessors/successors)

Creating calendars

Setting baselines

🔸 Top free tutorials:

YouTube Channel: “Oracle Primavera P6 Tutorials” by Plan Academy, Ten Six, or Project Controls Online

Blog: projectcontrolacademy.com – Free articles and videos

PDF Guides: Search "Primavera P6 Beginners Guide PDF" – Many free PDFs online

🔹 4. Practice Hands-On
Build a sample project (e.g., house construction, IT rollout, or road project). Include:

At least 3 levels of WBS

20–30 activities with dependencies

3 calendars (standard, 24-hr, night shift)

Basic resources (labour, material, cost)

Assign costs and generate a baseline

🔹 5. Learn Intermediate Skills
Once you’re comfortable:

Resource leveling

Project updating and progress tracking

Earned Value Management (EVM)

Filters, layouts, and reports

Import/export to Excel or XML

🔹 6. Get Certified (Optional but Helpful)
Oracle University offers a Primavera P6 certification

Udemy has high-quality Primavera courses (e.g., by CEM Solutions, Plan Academy) often under $15
