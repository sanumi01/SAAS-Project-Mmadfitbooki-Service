With SQL and Power BI certification, some of the best job roles you could consider include:

1. Data Analyst:
=================
   This role involves analyzing data, generating reports, and visualizing data using Power BI, 
   which is a perfect fit for your skills.

2. Business Intelligence Analyst:
==================================
 In this position, you would focus on interpreting complex data and providing insights to help businesses make informed 
decisions.

3. Data Scientist:
===================
 While this role may require additional skills in statistics and machine learning, having SQL and Power BI knowledge is a 
great foundation for data manipulation and visualization.

4. Database Administrator:
==========================
 This role involves managing and maintaining databases, where SQL skills are essential for querying and managing data.

5. Reporting Analyst: This job focuses on generating reports and dashboards, primarily using Power BI to present data 
in a clear and actionable manner.

Overall, roles that involve data analysis, reporting, and business intelligence would be highly suitable for someone 
with SQL and Power BI certifications.
